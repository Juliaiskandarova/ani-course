Approximation of the nonlinear dependence of the mechanical characteristic of an electric motor using a neural network method

Abstract

Introduction. In recent years, artificial neural networks have been used to solve many applied problems of various orientations. Neural networks can be used to model the behavior of physical systems, predict process dynamics, analyze experimental data, and optimize physical processes. The use of neural networks can be useful for modeling various phenomena, from simple physical models to complex nonlinear systems describing, for example, the behavior of a composite mechanism.

Materials and methods. In this work, the effectiveness of the neural network approach is demonstrated for the model of an asynchronous electric motor AIR56A2. Motors of this type are well compatible with pumping equipment used in the complex mechanical structure of such structures as hydraulic drives of drawbridges. Using a neural network, the mechanical characteristic of the motor was approximated, which is the dependence of the developed torque on the rotational speed. The obtained results are compared with the approximation of this dependence using a parabola constructed using the classical statistical least squares method. Further, the obtained approximations are used in the numerical solution by the Euler method for the nonlinear differential equation describing the dynamics of the motor, and the results are subsequently evaluated using a predetermined value of the steady-state angular velocity for this motor.

Results of the study. Numerical experiments demonstrate the achievement of a significant difference in results when using a multilayer perceptron for such a problem compared with the classical approach.

Discussion and conclusion. Due to the fact that the neural network approximates the mechanical characteristic substantially more accurately, the neural network approach allows obtaining more accurate results of mathematical modeling of the motor itself.

Keywords: mechanical characteristic of the motor, asynchronous motor, least squares approximation, Euler method, best linear unbiased estimates, Gauss-Markov conditions, multilayer perceptron, multilayer neural network, activation function, loss function

The differential equation of electric motor dynamics is solved

J · dω/dt = M_d − (M_n + M_u)   (1)

The problem includes the approximation of the mechanical characteristic of the motor. The mechanical characteristic of an electric motor is the dependence of its rotational speed n on the torque M developed by it, that is, n = f(M), described in the work. Usually, to approximate it, a parabola with unknown coefficients is chosen:

M_d = aω² + bω + c,   ω₁ < ω < ω₂.   (2)

We will compare this approximation with the neural network one.

Equation (1) describes, for example, the dynamics of the hydraulic drive of a drawbridge [14]. In this problem, the total moment of resistances applied to the motor will consist of M_n, M_u – torques on the shafts of the pump and the control pump.

where J is the reduced moment of inertia of the rotating masses of the electric motor, coupling, and pumps;
M_d is the driving torque of the electric motor;
M_n, M_u are the torques on the shafts of the pump and the control pump;
a, b, c are the coefficients of approximation of the mechanical characteristic of the electric motor.

As initial conditions, we take: at t = 0 ω = ω₀, where ω₀ is the angular velocity of the motor, taken as the initial condition for the solution of the system and equal to the minimum value of the stable speed at start-up.

In this work, the torques on the shafts of the pump and the control pump are taken as constants.

As the motor model under study, an asynchronous electric motor of the AIR56A2 model was taken. This model is often found in most automation systems in various fields of industry, and is also capable of performing lifting work and is compatible with pumping equipment.

The electric motor is equipped with a stator that creates a magnetic field using copper enameled wire. The magnetic field drives the rotor into rotation. The windings are connected to a power source that provides the supply of electric current to the system.

The technical characteristics of this model will subsequently be used to construct the mechanical characteristic of the motor, which will be approximated by the classical least squares method in the form of a parabola and by a neural network.
