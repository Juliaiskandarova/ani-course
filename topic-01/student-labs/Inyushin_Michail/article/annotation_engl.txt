The article examines the principles and tools that allow to optimize marketing strategies using machine learning algorithms and technologies It analyses the features of machine learning methods that allow to interpret users' actions based on their interaction with a product or service The author identifies that optimizing advertising budgets with the use of machine learning algorithms provides companies with tools for effective use of marketing resources increasing return on investment and achieving business goals The application of machine learning methods in Internet marketing is emphasised as an evolutionary step in digital business allowing companies to gain competitive advantages
